About OpenGeoStreams

OpenGeoStreams enables users to upload water-quality data for any river as a CSV and transform it into interactive maps, temporal trends, distribution summaries, precipitation comparisons, interpolation surfaces, and river-health insights. These visualizations help users explore spatial and seasonal patterns, identify potential water-quality concerns, and better understand the overall health of the river.
