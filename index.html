Explore station-wise measurements, scrub through the timeline, and compare parameter trends across the mapped sampling locations.

N-choe is a seasonal urban drain-stream system flowing through Chandigarh and Mohali before joining the Ghaggar, making it an important corridor for tracking wastewater influence and downstream water quality.
